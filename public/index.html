The science

Why ketamine works when nothing else has

Traditional antidepressants target serotonin — and take 4-8 weeks to work, if they work at all. Ketamine acts on glutamate receptors, triggering rapid neuroplasticity and building new neural pathways within hours.

For people who have tried everything, ketamine offers a fundamentally different mechanism — one that doesn't depend on the same pathways that have failed before.

Questions

Common questions

Is ketamine therapy legal?
Yes. Ketamine is FDA-approved as an anesthetic and has been used in medicine for over 50 years. When prescribed by a licensed physician for off-label psychiatric use, it is fully legal in all 50 states.
How is the medication delivered?
After physician approval, your prescription is fulfilled by a licensed U.S. pharmacy and shipped discreetly to your home. Most patients receive their medication within 3-5 business days.
What does treatment cost?
Most patients start around $150-200/month for a maintenance protocol. A DemaFora membership ($50/mo) provides significant savings across all programs.
Is it covered by insurance?
Most insurance plans do not cover compounded ketamine. DemaFora You is designed to be far more affordable than in-clinic IV ketamine infusions, which typically cost $400-$800 per session.
What are the side effects?
At low therapeutic doses, ketamine is well-tolerated. Temporary effects may include mild dissociation, drowsiness, or nausea. Your physician will walk you through exactly what to expect before you begin.
Will I need to stop my current medications?
Not necessarily. Many patients continue existing medications during ketamine therapy. Your physician will review all current medications during your consultation and advise accordingly.
