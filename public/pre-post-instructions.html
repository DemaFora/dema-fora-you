Pre and Post-Treatment Instructions

Follow these guidelines for every session

← Back to Documents

These instructions apply to every ketamine therapy session. Following them carefully will help ensure your safety and the effectiveness of your treatment.

Before Your Session
  • Fast for at least 4 hours before your session — no food or heavy liquids
  • Clear liquids (water, clear broth) are allowed up to 2 hours before
  • Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours before your session
  • Avoid recreational substances for at least 72 hours
  • Take your usual medications unless your provider has told you otherwise
  • Wear comfortable, loose clothing
  • Arrange a trusted person to be present or available during your session
  • Arrange transportation home — do not drive yourself
  • Prepare your space: quiet, dimly lit, comfortable temperature
  • Have an eye mask and headphones available if desired
After Your Session
  • Rest for the remainder of the day
  • Do not drive or operate machinery for at least 24 hours
  • Do not make important decisions for 24 hours
  • Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours after your session
  • Eat lightly — begin with bland foods if you feel nauseous
  • Stay hydrated
  • Journal your experience while it is fresh — this supports integration
  • Contact your care coordinator if you have concerns or unexpected symptoms
  • Avoid stressful environments or situations for the remainder of the day
Do not drive. You may feel fine after a session but your judgment, reflexes, and perception may remain impaired for up to 24 hours. This is a strict safety requirement, not a suggestion.

Medications to Discuss with Your Provider

Certain medications may interact with ketamine therapy. Do not stop any prescription medication on your own. Discuss the following with your provider before your first session:

  • Benzodiazepines (Xanax, Valium, Klonopin, Ativan) — may reduce effectiveness
  • MAOIs — potentially dangerous combination
  • Stimulants (Adderall, Ritalin) — discuss timing and dose
  • Opioids — disclose all current use
  • Lamotrigine (Lamictal) — may reduce effectiveness
  • Any new or recently changed prescriptions

Integration — After Your Series

Ketamine therapy creates a window of neuroplasticity — your brain is more receptive to change after treatment. Making the most of this window significantly affects outcomes.

Your provider may recommend working with a therapist or integration coach between sessions. This is not required but is associated with better long-term results. Ask your care coordinator about integration support options.
  • Journal regularly in the days following each session
  • Engage in therapy or counseling if you have access to it
  • Prioritize sleep, movement, and reduced stress
  • Limit alcohol and substance use during the treatment series

When to Contact Your Care Team

  • Symptoms lasting more than 24 hours after a session
  • Significant mood changes, new or worsening thoughts of self-harm
  • Confusion, memory loss, or difficulty functioning
  • Any unusual physical symptoms

For emergencies, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. For mental health crises, call or text 988.
