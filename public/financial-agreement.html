Program Pricing

Pricing is provided at the time of enrollment and confirmed with your care coordinator. The following reflects current standard program fees. Fees are subject to change with notice.

ServiceDescriptionFee
Initial ConsultationMedical eligibility evaluation with licensed providerIncluded
Initial Treatment SeriesTypically 6 sessions over 2-3 weeksPer program quote
Individual Maintenance SessionOngoing sessions as clinically indicatedPer program quote
Integration SupportOptional add-on with integration specialistPer program quote
Prescription Medication (if applicable)Separate from program feesBilled separately

Exact pricing will be confirmed in writing prior to your first paid session. You will not be billed without prior authorization.

Payment Terms

  • Payment is due at the time of service unless a payment plan has been arranged in writing
  • We accept major credit and debit cards and ACH bank transfer
  • Payment plans are available — ask your care coordinator for details
  • We do not bill insurance directly. A superbill can be provided for potential out-of-network reimbursement upon request — reimbursement is not guaranteed
  • HSA and FSA cards are generally accepted — verify with your plan administrator

Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

  • Cancellations made more than 48 hours before your scheduled session will not be charged
  • Cancellations within 48 hours of your session may be subject to a late cancellation fee
  • No-shows (failure to attend without notice) will be billed the full session fee
  • Exceptions may be made for documented medical emergencies at our discretion
  • To cancel or reschedule, contact your care coordinator as early as possible

Refund Policy

  • Fees paid for completed sessions are non-refundable
  • If you prepay for a session package and discontinue treatment, unused sessions will be refunded within 14 business days, less any administrative fees
  • Refund requests must be submitted in writing to your care coordinator
  • Initial consultation fees are non-refundable once the consultation has taken place

Discontinuation of Treatment

You may discontinue treatment at any time. Please notify your care coordinator so your provider can ensure a safe and appropriate transition of care. Abrupt discontinuation without notice may affect your access to records and follow-up care.

Billing Disputes

If you believe you have been billed in error, contact your care coordinator within 30 days of the charge. We will review and resolve billing disputes promptly.
