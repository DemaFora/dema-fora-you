Introduction

This document explains the nature of ketamine therapy, its potential benefits and risks, and your rights as a patient. By signing below, you confirm that you have read, understood, and voluntarily agree to proceed with treatment.

Ketamine is an FDA-approved anesthetic that has been studied and used clinically for treatment-resistant depression, anxiety disorders, PTSD, and chronic pain. Treatment is administered under the supervision of a licensed medical provider through our licensed provider network.

What Is Ketamine Therapy

Ketamine therapy involves the administration of ketamine at sub-anesthetic doses to produce therapeutic effects. Sessions typically involve an altered state of consciousness lasting 45 to 60 minutes. Treatment is delivered in a monitored setting or through a supervised at-home protocol, as determined by your provider.

A typical course of treatment consists of an initial series of sessions followed by maintenance sessions as clinically indicated.

Potential Benefits

  • Rapid reduction in symptoms of depression, including treatment-resistant depression
  • Reduction in anxiety and PTSD symptoms
  • Reduction in chronic pain
  • Improved mood and quality of life
  • Effects may be experienced within hours to days of initial treatment

Results vary by individual. No guarantee of specific outcomes is made or implied.

Known Risks and Side Effects

As with any medical treatment, ketamine therapy carries potential risks. You should discuss any concerns with your provider before proceeding.

During and immediately after treatment:

  • Dissociation, perceptual disturbances, or altered sense of time
  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Increased blood pressure or heart rate
  • Dizziness, drowsiness, or difficulty concentrating
  • Emotional release or psychological discomfort
  • Blurred or double vision

Less common risks:

  • Emergence of agitation or anxiety during the session
  • Temporary memory impairment
  • In rare cases, allergic reaction

Long-term considerations:

  • Psychological dependence in rare cases, particularly with frequent high-dose use
  • Bladder complications have been reported with chronic recreational misuse at high doses — not associated with supervised therapeutic use at clinical doses
Important: You must arrange transportation home after every ketamine session. You may not drive or operate heavy machinery for at least 24 hours following treatment. Failure to arrange transportation may result in postponement of your session.

Contraindications

Ketamine therapy may not be appropriate if you have any of the following conditions. Disclose your full medical history to your provider:

  • Uncontrolled hypertension or cardiovascular disease
  • Active psychosis or personal or family history of schizophrenia
  • History of substance use disorder (evaluated case by case)
  • Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  • Hyperthyroidism
  • Elevated intracranial or intraocular pressure
  • Severe liver disease
  • Current use of certain medications (discuss with your provider)

Alternatives to Treatment

Alternatives to ketamine therapy include traditional antidepressant medications, psychotherapy (including CBT and DBT), electroconvulsive therapy (ECT), transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS), and other evidence-based approaches. You are not required to pursue ketamine therapy and may choose any available treatment option.

Confidentiality

Your health information is protected under HIPAA. Please review the separate Notice of Privacy Practices. In limited circumstances required by law, your provider may be obligated to disclose information (e.g., imminent risk of harm to self or others).

Your Rights

  • You have the right to ask questions at any time before or during treatment.
  • You have the right to withdraw consent and discontinue treatment at any time without penalty.
  • You have the right to a second opinion before proceeding.
  • You have the right to receive a copy of this signed consent form.
