What Is Telehealth

Telehealth involves the delivery of health care services using electronic communications, including video conferencing, telephone, and secure messaging. DemaFora You uses telehealth for initial consultations, follow-up appointments, and ongoing care coordination through our licensed provider network.

How Telehealth Works

Your consultations will be conducted via HIPAA-compliant video or audio platform. You will receive a secure link or instructions prior to your scheduled appointment. You are responsible for having a private space, a stable internet or phone connection, and any required device.

Benefits of Telehealth

  • Access to care from your home or any private location
  • Reduced travel time and cost
  • Greater scheduling flexibility
  • Continuity of care between in-person sessions where applicable

Risks and Limitations

  • Technical failures (audio, video, connection) may interrupt or delay a session
  • Telehealth may not be appropriate for all clinical situations — your provider will advise if in-person care is necessary
  • In emergencies, telehealth cannot substitute for in-person emergency care
  • Information transmitted electronically carries inherent security risks despite safeguards

Privacy and Security

All telehealth sessions are conducted on HIPAA-compliant platforms. Sessions are not recorded without your explicit written consent. You should conduct appointments from a private location to protect your own confidentiality. Do not use public Wi-Fi for telehealth appointments.

State Licensing

Telehealth services are provided by licensed providers who are authorized to practice in your state of residence. You are responsible for informing your care team if you relocate or travel to a different state, as licensure requirements may affect your ability to receive services.

Emergency Protocol

If you experience a medical or mental health emergency during or between sessions, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room immediately. Telehealth is not an emergency service. For mental health crises, you may also call or text 988 (Suicide and Crisis Lifeline).

Please provide your current address and emergency contact information before your first session so your care team can coordinate emergency assistance if needed.

Your Rights

  • You may withdraw consent for telehealth at any time and request in-person alternatives where available.
  • You have the right to be informed of any technical limitations before your session begins.
  • You have the right to privacy during sessions.

Patient Signature

By signing, I confirm I have read and understand this telehealth consent and voluntarily agree to receive services via telehealth.
