Notice of Privacy Practices

HIPAA — How we use and protect your health information

← Back to Documents
This notice describes how medical information about you may be used and disclosed and how you can get access to this information. Please review it carefully.

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

DemaFora You and its licensed provider network are committed to protecting the privacy of your health information. We are required by law to maintain the privacy of your protected health information (PHI), provide you with this notice of our legal duties and privacy practices, and follow the terms of the notice currently in effect.

How We May Use and Disclose Your Information

Treatment: We may use and share your health information to provide, coordinate, and manage your care, including sharing with your treating providers, pharmacies, laboratories, and other professionals involved in your treatment.

Payment: We may use and share your health information to bill and receive payment for services, including communication with insurers or payment processors where applicable.

Health Care Operations: We may use your information for quality improvement, training, compliance, and administrative purposes necessary to operate our practice.

Other Permitted Disclosures

  • As required by law — court orders, subpoenas, or other legal process
  • Public health activities — reporting certain diseases or conditions to public health authorities
  • Abuse or neglect — reporting suspected abuse to appropriate authorities
  • Safety — to prevent or lessen a serious and imminent threat to health or safety
  • Research — under strict protocols with appropriate privacy protections
  • Organ donation — to organ procurement organizations as authorized
  • Military/veterans — if you are a member of the armed forces, as required by military command
  • Workers compensation — as authorized by law

Uses Requiring Your Authorization

For uses not described above, we will ask for your written authorization, including:

  • Most disclosures of psychotherapy notes
  • Marketing uses
  • Sale of your health information
  • Any other use not covered by this notice

You may revoke a written authorization at any time, except where we have already relied on it.

Your Rights

  • Right to access: You may request a copy of your health record. We may charge a reasonable fee.
  • Right to amend: You may request corrections to your health record.
  • Right to an accounting: You may request a list of certain disclosures of your information.
  • Right to restrict: You may request limits on how we use or share your information.
  • Right to confidential communications: You may request that we contact you in a specific way.
  • Right to a paper copy of this notice: You may request a printed copy at any time.
  • Right to file a complaint: If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, you may file a complaint with us or with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights. We will not retaliate against you for filing a complaint.

Changes to This Notice

We reserve the right to change the terms of this notice and to make the new notice effective for all protected health information we maintain. The current notice is always available upon request.

Contact Us

For questions about this notice or to exercise any of your rights, contact our Privacy Officer through your care coordinator or via the contact information provided at the time of enrollment.
